# Record your part in two minutes

You read along with a video. Nothing to memorise.

1. Lab coat on. Quiet spot, light on your face.
2. Open **your** read-along video from `pitch/prompter/` on a laptop, volume up:
   - Victor: `prompter-1-victor.mp4`, then `prompter-5-close-victor.mp4`
   - Duc: `prompter-2-duc.mp4`
   - Joseph: `prompter-3-joseph.mp4`
   - Stef: `prompter-4-stef.mp4`
3. Prop your phone **landscape** just above the laptop screen, your head in the **centre** of the frame.
4. **Start the phone recording first.** Then press play on the laptop.
5. You'll hear three beeps and see 3-2-1. On the **high beep**, start reading the subtitle. The **pink dot** shows which word to be on. Keep up with the dot.
6. Stop recording when the clip ends. Do it twice; send both.
7. AirDrop to Victor as `talk-<yourname>.mp4`.

Keep the laptop sound on: the beeps on your recording are how your video gets lined up with the film. Don't worry about small stumbles.
