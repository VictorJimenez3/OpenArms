A simulated experiment adapter, compatible request scopes, reset/evaluation harness, and separate simulated promotion policy remain necessary. The inspected integration review records checkout `c1f60be3b3e800fa5e78b6fba427eefc25ab80b4`; it does not establish support implemented elsewhere. See [stack integration review](../../visual_assets/training_scene/INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md) and `docs/INTERFACES.md`.

Candidate definitions are evaluator-only configuration, not policy inputs. Expected outcomes, hidden state, oracle sidecars, and art HUDs must stay outside camera-only policy observations. Density and indicator responses require separately versioned analytical models labeled MODEL-PREDICTED; rendering their outputs does not independently validate buoyancy or chemistry. Manipulation success, simulated measurements, and model predictions need distinct evidence. Ramp/lever behavior additionally requires actual contacts/joints rather than illustrative animation. Proposed tolerances and episode counts remain plans, not measured capabilities or completed trials.
