# OpenArms

## Language

**Student-directed action**: An individual physical action chosen by the student. The student decides the sequence.

**Reusable skill**: A tested, parameterized capability that can serve later requests without repeating unnecessary reasoning.

**Harness improvement**: An evidence-backed change to tools, algorithms, perception or execution that improves capability, speed or reliability while preserving required behavior.

**Fast reuse**: Invoking an established capability with less planning effort. This is distinct from improving its implementation.

**Physical demo**: The live student-to-robot experience on the actual bench. Simulation is preparation evidence.
